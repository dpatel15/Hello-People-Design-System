# Hello People — Voice & Tone

The way we sound is half the brand. A calm, human voice is what makes an AI
company feel trustworthy instead of intimidating.

---

## Voice (always) vs. Tone (adapts)

**Voice** is fixed: plain-spoken, confident, human, credible.
**Tone** flexes to the moment — warmer on a landing page, more precise in docs,
more reassuring in an error message.

---

## Five rules

1. **Sell the outcome, not the technology.** Nobody buys "LLM-powered workflow
   orchestration." They buy *"get your evenings back."* Lead with the result.
2. **Plain language, always.** If a smart 12-year-old wouldn't follow it, rewrite
   it. Explain the hard thing simply — that's the flex.
3. **Talk to one person.** "You," not "clients" or "users." Second person, active
   voice, present tense.
4. **Be confident, not hypey.** State what's true. Drop "revolutionary,"
   "cutting-edge," "game-changing," "seamless," "synergy," "unlock," "supercharge."
5. **Respect their time.** Short sentences. Front-load the point. Cut throat-clearing.

---

## Before → After

| ❌ Don't | ✅ Do |
|---|---|
| Leverage cutting-edge LLM-powered synergies to transform your workflow | Turn a 2-day job into a 2-hour one. |
| Our revolutionary AI-driven paradigm will disrupt your industry | We watch how you work, then automate the boring parts. |
| Solutions tailored to your specific vertical | Any business. We'll find what's worth automating. |
| Please be advised that submission was unsuccessful | That didn't send. Check your email address and try again. |
| Utilize our platform to optimize operational efficiency | Do more of the work that matters. We'll handle the rest. |

---

## Words we use / avoid

**Use:** automate, busywork, hours back, in plain English, we'll handle it, how you
actually work, the boring parts, real, simple, people.

**Avoid:** revolutionary, cutting-edge, game-changer, disrupt, synergy, seamless,
robust, leverage (as a verb), unlock, supercharge, next-gen, "AI-powered"
(as a crutch).

---

## Tone by context

- **Landing/hero:** warm, bold, benefit-first. Short. One clear promise.
  → *"More done. More human. We automate the busywork so your team can focus on people."*
- **Service pages:** confident and concrete. Name the task, name the time saved.
- **Proposals:** credible and specific. Numbers, timelines, plain scope. No fluff.
- **Errors:** calm, human, useful. Say what happened and how to fix it. Never blame
  the user, never apologize twice.
- **Social:** friendly and useful. Teach something small, show a real before/after.
  Sound like a person, not a brand account.
- **Buttons/CTAs:** a verb + the outcome. *"Book an automation audit,"* *"Hear a
  demo,"* *"See what we'd automate."* Never just "Submit" or "Learn more."

---

## Micro-copy checklist

- [ ] Would a busy owner understand this in one read?
- [ ] Is there exactly one clear next action?
- [ ] Did I remove every buzzword?
- [ ] Does it sound like a helpful person, not a pitch?
- [ ] Is the benefit before the mechanism?
