# Hello People Social

Two families, one brand. Templates in `web/`; ready-to-post PNG exports and
reusable background SVGs in `assets/social/`.

- **Minimal kit** (`web/social.html`): clean light/dark posts. Solid blue carries
  the work, gradient reserved for one moment. Best for tips, offers, stats.
- **Structured kit** (`web/social-structured.html`): the warped-grid look. Highlight
  the key words in a blue block, faint blue grid + soft ribbons, consistent
  carousel scaffold. Best for carousels, testimonials, memes.

Overview of every type: `assets/social/_overview.png`.

## The reusable background (structured kit)

`assets/social/backgrounds/hello-people-bg-grid.svg` (grid only) and
`hello-people-bg-grid-ribbons.svg` (grid + ribbons). Both bake in the fade, so the
warped grid and blue ribbons sit in a top band and a mirrored bottom band and
fade into white at the left and right edges, leaving the middle clean for content.
Drop either behind any post and place your copy in the center.

## The highlighter (structured kit)

The signature move: wrap the key words in a solid blue block with white text
(`.hl`). Reserve the gradient highlight (`.hlg`) for one hero word on a cover only.
Their yellow-with-black becomes our blue-with-white (blue is dark, so text flips
to white). Emphasis words can also be bold or bold-italic Poppins.

## Rules (so every post looks like us)

1. **One idea per post.** If it needs two headlines, it is two posts.
2. **Gradient is rare.** Minimal kit follows 95/5. Structured kit uses solid-blue
   highlights; the gradient highlight is a cover-only treat.
3. **Poppins for the statement, Inter for the rest.** Big, confident headline.
4. **Voice.** Plain and human, sell the outcome, no buzzwords, no dashes. See
   `voice-and-tone.md`.
5. **Always sign off** with the logo and `@hellopeople_agency` (or `hellopeople.ca`).
6. **Safe zone.** On Stories/Reels keep text clear of the top and bottom ~250px.
7. **Photos:** background-removed, black-and-white cut-out with a subtle white
   outline, placed at the bottom (see the Testimonial and Meme templates).
8. **Contrast holds.** White or ink text only on solid color or the gradient,
   never small text on a busy image.
