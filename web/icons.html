One grid, one stroke, one system

All icons are 24px, 2px stroke, round caps and joins, and drawn in currentColor so they inherit text color and theme. Line style only. Use these brand icons for our signature concepts, and Lucide for everything else, it shares the same grid.

Rules

How to use them

24px grid, 2px strokeRound caps and joins. Never rescale the stroke, resize the whole icon.
currentColor onlyThe icon takes the surrounding text color, so it works in light, dark, and on solid blue.
Line, not fillKeep icons outline-only. A single icon may take the gradient for one hero moment, like the logo.
Pair with a labelIcons support words, they do not replace them. Never color-alone for meaning.
