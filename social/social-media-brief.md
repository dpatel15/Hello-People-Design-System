# Hello People, social media brief

A single, self-contained handoff. Give this whole document to a designer, a
copywriter, or an AI model, and they should have everything they need to
create on-brand social posts without asking follow up questions.

Owner: Hello People (hellopeople.ca).
Handles: `@hellopeople_agency` on Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn; `@hellopeople` on X (fallback: use `hellopeople.ca` in the sign off).
Contact: info@hellopeople.ca.
Design system: this repo (`Hello-People-Design-System`). All values below come from it.

---

## 0. The one non-negotiable writing rule

**Never use the em dash (`,`) or the en dash (`-`) anywhere.** The client
finds them AI-generated. Use a comma, colon, period, or parentheses to break a
sentence. Write "to" for ranges (Mon to Fri, 120 to 320ms, 8 to 5). A plain
hyphen "-" in compound words (follow-ups, on-brand, two-hour) is fine.

If a caption, headline, or button label contains `,` or `-`, it is off brand
and must be rewritten.

## 1. Who we are (context so decisions make sense)

**Hello People** is an AI automation and growth agency. In plain words: we
help businesses hand their busywork to machines so their people can do the
work only people can do.

- Automation: workflow automation, AI voice agents, phone and booking agents,
  reporting, monitoring. We watch how a business actually works, find the
  repetitive multi-day tasks, and rebuild them as systems that run in hours.
- Growth and social: content creation, video editing, Google and Meta ads, SEO.

We are industry agnostic. Any business has busywork; we find what is worth
automating and build it.

### The one idea

**Human on the surface. Engineered underneath.**

The name is a greeting. The work is machine intelligence. That tension is the
brand: we are the warm face on powerful automation. Every post should feel
that way, human on top, quietly precise underneath.

### Positioning

For business owners drowning in repetitive work, Hello People is the AI
partner that turns two-day tasks into two-hour ones, combining real technical
capability with a human, jargon-free way of working. Unlike typical AI vendors
who sell buzzwords and black boxes, we start by understanding how you actually
work, then automate the parts that do not need a human.

### Taglines

- **Primary tagline (locked):** Say hello to less busywork.
- **Supporting descriptor:** We automate the busywork, so your team can focus on the work that needs a human.
- **Mission line (about us):** Bridging humans and AI.
- **Rotating campaign lines:** More done. More human. / Your business, minus the busywork. / Automate the busywork. Keep the human. / The human side of automation. / AI that gives you your time back.

### Audience

Small and mid business owners and ops leads. Busy, practical, skeptical of AI
hype, curious about real outcomes. They want their evenings back, not a
technical demo.

## 2. Personality

| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| Warm, human, approachable | Cutesy, casual, unserious |
| Confident and plain-spoken | Salesy, hypey, buzzword-heavy |
| Premium, restrained, considered | Flashy, cluttered, gimmicky |
| Precise and credible | Cold, robotic, "sci-fi AI" |
| Optimistic about what people do | Fear-mongering about jobs |

If the brand were a person: a sharp, calm expert who explains hard things
simply, respects your time, and clearly knows their craft, not the loud one
in the room.

### How a post should FEEL, in order

1. **Calm** (relief). Space, restraint, one idea. AI is overwhelming; our
   posts should feel like a deep breath.
2. **Trust** (precision). Perfect alignment, real hierarchy, nothing sloppy.
   Trust is earned in the details before a single word is read.
3. **Warmth** (human). Copy that talks to one person, not at a crowd. Magenta
   or a friendly line shows the humans behind the tech.
4. **Clarity to momentum**. One clear next step per post. No maze of buttons
   or CTAs.

If a post makes someone feel anxious, confused, or "marketed at", it has
failed, no matter how nice it looks.

## 3. Voice and tone

Voice is fixed. Tone flexes.

### Five rules

1. **Sell the outcome, not the technology.** Nobody buys "LLM-powered
   workflow orchestration." They buy "get your evenings back."
2. **Plain language, always.** If a smart 12 year old would not follow it,
   rewrite it. Explaining a hard thing simply is the flex.
3. **Talk to one person.** "You," not "clients" or "users." Second person,
   active voice, present tense.
4. **Be confident, not hypey.** State what is true. Drop revolutionary,
   cutting-edge, game-changing, seamless, synergy, unlock, supercharge.
5. **Respect their time.** Short sentences. Front-load the point. Cut
   throat-clearing.
6. **No em or en dashes.** Ever. See section 0.

### Words we use

automate, busywork, hours back, in plain English, we will handle it, how you
actually work, the boring parts, real, simple, people.

### Words we avoid

revolutionary, cutting-edge, game-changer, disrupt, synergy, seamless,
robust, leverage (as a verb), unlock, supercharge, next-gen, "AI-powered"
(as a crutch).

### Before to after examples

| Do not write | Write instead |
|---|---|
| Leverage cutting-edge LLM-powered synergies to transform your workflow | Turn a 2 day job into a 2 hour one. |
| Our revolutionary AI-driven paradigm will disrupt your industry | We watch how you work, then automate the boring parts. |
| Solutions tailored to your specific vertical | Any business. We will find what is worth automating. |
| Please be advised that submission was unsuccessful | That did not send. Check your email address and try again. |
| Utilize our platform to optimize operational efficiency | Do more of the work that matters. We will handle the rest. |

### Tone by context

- **Landing or hero copy:** warm, bold, benefit first. Short. One clear promise.
- **Service pages:** confident and concrete. Name the task, name the time saved.
- **Errors:** calm, human, useful. Say what happened and how to fix it.
- **Social:** friendly and useful. Teach something small, show a real before or after. Sound like a person, not a brand account.
- **CTAs:** verb + outcome. "Book an automation audit." "Hear a demo." "See what we would automate." Never just "Submit" or "Learn more."

### Micro-copy checklist (run this on every caption)

- [ ] Would a busy owner understand this in one read?
- [ ] Is there exactly one clear next action?
- [ ] Did I remove every buzzword?
- [ ] Does it sound like a helpful person, not a pitch?
- [ ] Is the benefit before the mechanism?
- [ ] Zero em or en dashes?

## 4. Colors (exact values, do not swap)

We use three brand colors on a large, calm neutral canvas. Restraint is the
point.

### Brand core

| Color | Hex | Feels like | Use for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Link Blue** | `#1D50CF` | Trust, intelligence, stability. "The tech is solid." | Primary actions, links, active states, focus rings, data. The default brand color when you need one. |
| **Bridge Violet** | `#903DA4` | Transition, the meeting point. | Mostly inside the gradient. Rarely used solid. |
| **People Magenta** | `#E0497C` | Warmth, energy, humanity. | Human touches, a highlight, an underline, the period in the wordmark, one small emphasis per view. |
| **Ink 800** | `#1B1E27` | Grounded, premium, serious. | Headlines, dominant text. |
| **Ink 600** | `#3F454C` | Softer than pure black. | Body text on light. Matches the "Hello" in the wordmark. |

### Support (dark mode variants of the brand core)

- Blue: `#3D6DE4`
- Violet: `#A84EBD`
- Magenta: `#EC5C90`

### Neutrals (cool biased, feel chosen not default grey)

`#FAFAFC` paper, `#F4F4F8` surface, `#E6E7EE` hairline border,
`#C9CCD5`, `#9195A3`, `#6B6F7D` muted text, `#565B6A`, `#3F454C`,
`#1B1E27` ink, `#14161D`, `#0C0D12`.

### Feedback (for tips, warnings, before or after markers)

- Success `#12A150` on `#E7F6EE`
- Warning `#C77700` on `#FBF0DC`
- Danger `#E5484D` on `#FCE9E9`
- Info `#1D50CF` on `#E4EAF9`

### The signature gradient

`linear-gradient(120deg, #1D50CF 0%, #903DA4 52%, #E0497C 100%)`

It runs blue to violet to magenta: cool to warm, machine to human. That
direction is the story. Never reverse it.

### The 95 / 5 rule (the single most important design rule)

- Roughly **95%** of a post is flat: paper, ink, or solid blue.
- The gradient earns the other **~5%**, and only in these reserved places:
  - The **logo** (already contains the gradient).
  - **One** hero brand moment per piece (a payoff panel, one key graphic).
  - The **closing CTA slide** of a carousel, or a CTA hero image.
- Everything else (buttons, headlines, links, icons, numbers, stats) uses
  **solid Link Blue** `#1D50CF`.

Gradient is banned on:
- Full-page or full-section backgrounds (only exception: one CTA slide).
- Buttons, body copy, small text.
- More than one gradient moment competing in the same view.

If in doubt, use solid blue.

### Accessibility (non-negotiable)

- Body text meets WCAG AA (contrast ratio at least 4.5 to 1). Ink `#1B1E27` on
  paper `#FAFAFC` clears this easily.
- Magenta and blue are fine on large text, icons, and fills. Check contrast
  before using either on small text on white (magenta on white is borderline
  for body sizes).
- Never put small text directly on the gradient. White on the gradient is
  fine at display sizes only.
- Color is never the only signal (pair with icon, label, or weight).

## 5. Typography

Two families with clear jobs.

- **Poppins**, display / headlines. Rounded, geometric, friendly. Echoes the
  logo directly.
  - Weights we use: **800 (Extra Bold)** for social headlines, **700 (Bold)**
    for site headings, **600 (SemiBold)** for subheads.
  - Slightly tight tracking on large sizes.
- **Inter**, body and UI. Neutral, highly legible.
  - Weights: **400** body, **500 or 600** for UI labels and emphasis.
  - Line height 1.5 to 1.7 on body text.
- **JetBrains Mono**, only for code, tokens, or metrics that should read
  "engineered". Optional, use lightly.

### Hierarchy rules

- One headline per post. Do not stack two large statements.
- Uppercase eyebrows or labels get wide tracking (letter spacing ~0.16em) and
  a muted color.
- Numbers that line up in columns use tabular figures.
- Size and weight create hierarchy, NOT color. Save color for meaning.
- Stay on the type scale (roughly 12, 14, 16, 18, 20, 24, 30, 36, 48, 60, 72
  px), do not invent one-off sizes.

## 6. The logo

Files live in `assets/logo/`. Always use the SVGs, never redraw or re-typeset.

- **Lockup (mark + wordmark), light backgrounds:** `hello-people-logo.svg`
- **Lockup, dark backgrounds:** `hello-people-logo-dark.svg`
- **One color white** (photos, merch, busy backgrounds): `hello-people-logo-white.svg`
- **One color black** (print, invoices): `hello-people-logo-black.svg`
- **Mark only:** `hello-people-mark.svg` (and `-dark`, `-white`, `-black`)
- **Favicons:** `hello-people-favicon.svg` and `-dark`

### Anatomy

- The mark is two speech bubbles: an open blue ring (the human) locking into a
  gradient bubble (the intelligence). Together, "bridging humans and AI."
- The wordmark is **Hello** (charcoal) stacked over **People.** (gradient) in
  Poppins Bold. The period is part of the logo. It makes it a statement.

### Rules

- **Clear space:** keep padding around the logo equal to the height of the
  "H" in "Hello" on all sides. Nothing intrudes.
- **Minimum size:** the horizontal lockup no smaller than 140px wide (about
  32px tall) on screen. The mark no smaller than 24px. Below that, use the
  mark.
- On photos or busy backgrounds, place the logo on a solid neutral chip, or
  use the white or black one-color version. Never let the gradient fight a
  busy image.
- **Never:** recolor the gradient, stretch, squash, rotate, skew, add drop
  shadows, glows, or outlines, put the charcoal lockup on a dark background,
  re-typeset the wordmark, or separate "People" from its period.

Every social post signs off with the logo and the handle. See section 8.

## 7. Social system: two families

We ship two kits. Pick one per post, do not mix them.

### Minimal kit (`web/social.html`)

Clean light or dark posts. Solid blue carries the work, gradient reserved for
one moment. Best for tips, offers, stats, LinkedIn.

### Structured kit (`web/social-structured.html`)

The warped-grid look. Highlight the key words in a solid blue block, faint
blue grid + soft ribbons background, consistent carousel scaffold. Best for
carousels, testimonials, memes, and anything that wants the extra "us" feel.

Overview of every type: `assets/social/_overview.png`. Ready-to-use examples:
`assets/social/examples/`.

### Formats (exact pixel sizes, do not resize down)

| Template | Family | Size (px) | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tip or educational post | Minimal | 1080 x 1080 | IG feed, FB |
| Before or after | Minimal | 1080 x 1080 | IG feed, FB |
| Offer or CTA | Minimal | 1080 x 1350 | IG feed (portrait) |
| Story or Reel cover | Minimal | 1080 x 1920 | IG story, Reels cover |
| LinkedIn | Minimal | 1200 x 627 | LinkedIn feed |
| Carousel cover, content, CTA | Structured | 1080 x 1350 | IG or LinkedIn carousels |
| Testimonial (with photo) | Structured | 1080 x 1350 | IG feed, LinkedIn |
| Context | Structured | 1080 x 1350 | IG feed |
| Meme | Structured | 1080 x 1350 | IG feed |

**Safe zone on Stories or Reels:** keep text clear of the top and bottom
~250 to 300 px so it does not clash with the platform UI.

### The reusable backgrounds (structured kit)

- `assets/social/backgrounds/hello-people-bg-grid.svg` (grid only)
- `assets/social/backgrounds/hello-people-bg-grid-ribbons.svg` (grid + ribbons)

Both fade to white at the left and right edges, leaving the middle clean for
content. A soft radial white wash sits over them for text legibility. Drop
either behind any post and place copy in the center.

### The highlighter (signature move)

Wrap the key words in a solid blue block with white text. This is the
brand's yellow-with-black moment, in Hello People colors.

- Solid blue highlight: `background: #1D50CF; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 0.04em 0.16em; border-radius: 8px;` (in code, that is the `.hl` class).
- Gradient highlight: reserved for **one** hero word on a carousel cover
  only, never anywhere else. Uses `--hp-gradient`.
- On the blue CTA slide, invert: white block with blue text.
- Never highlight more than one phrase per slide.
- Never highlight for more than about a third of the headline width.
- Bold or bold italic Poppins is an acceptable secondary emphasis.

### Contextual illustration rule (fills empty bands)

When a slide has an open area (under a numbered carousel point, next to a
CTA), drop in ONE faint line illustration that matches that slide's message,
drawn in the brand icon language:

- 24 x 24 viewBox, single-weight line icon, round caps and joins.
- Fixed treatment: **0.3px stroke, 15% opacity**, the same on light and dark
  slides.
- Color: brand blue on light backgrounds, white on blue backgrounds.
- Skip it where the slide is already full (a feed post with a full list, a
  before or after grid).

Use the icons we already have (see section 9), or draw a new one to match.

### Icons

24 brand icons in `assets/icons/brand/` (prefixed `hp-`) plus the full Lucide
library (2,025 icons) in `assets/icons/lucide/`. Browse them all in
`web/icons.html`.

- Grid: 24 x 24, 2px stroke, round caps and joins, line style only.
- `fill="none"` and `stroke="currentColor"` so the icon inherits color.
- Line only, not filled. A single hero icon may take the brand gradient for
  one hero moment (like the logo), never as the default.
- Always pair an icon with a label. Icons support words, they do not replace
  them.

Brand icons: `hp-workflow`, `hp-automate`, `hp-voice-agent`, `hp-phone-agent`,
`hp-chat-ai`, `hp-time-saved`, `hp-audit`, `hp-integration`, `hp-reporting`,
`hp-growth`, `hp-booking`, `hp-review`, `hp-email`, `hp-team`, `hp-launch`,
`hp-trust`, plus common UI icons.

### Photos (when we use them)

- Real people, real workplaces, natural light. Candid over posed.
- **No** glossy "robot handshake" or blue-glow circuit-board stock.
- In the Testimonial and Meme templates: background-removed, black-and-white
  cut-out with a subtle white outline, placed at the bottom.
- Contrast holds: white or ink text only on solid color or gradient, never
  small text on a busy image.

### Post sign off (every post)

Bottom of the layout: the Hello People logo on the left, the handle
`@hellopeople_agency` (or `hellopeople.ca` on platforms where the handle
differs) on the right, both in the muted grey. Use the white logo on the
blue CTA slide.

### The 9 hard rules

1. One idea per post. If it needs two headlines, it is two posts.
2. Gradient is rare (95 / 5 in the minimal kit; solid blue highlights in the
   structured kit; gradient highlight reserved for one cover word).
3. Poppins for the statement, Inter for the rest. Big, confident headline.
4. Voice: plain and human, sell the outcome, no buzzwords, no dashes.
5. Always sign off with the logo and `@hellopeople_agency`.
6. Safe zone: on Stories or Reels, keep text clear of the top and bottom ~250
   to 300px.
7. Photos: background-removed black-and-white cut-out with a subtle white
   outline, at the bottom (see the Testimonial and Meme templates).
8. Contrast holds. White or ink text on solid color or gradient only.
9. Contextual illustration fills empty bands (0.3px stroke, 15% opacity, one
   per slide).

## 8. Content strategy

### Content pillars (every topic maps to one; every week should use all four)

1. **Teach** (educational). How AI works in a real business day. Builds
   authority, earns saves and shares.
2. **Prove** (before or after, cases, numbers). Real outcomes. Builds trust.
3. **Bust** (myths and objections). "AI will not replace your team." Removes
   fear.
4. **Invite** (offers and lead magnets). "Comment AUDIT." Converts attention.

Weekly mix, rough: 40% Teach, 25% Prove, 20% Bust, 15% Invite.

### Post angle checklist (pick one per post)

Educational, before or after, how-to, objection, proof or case, relatable,
reflection.

### Topic bank (starter, rotate and add)

AI receptionist / missed calls, invoice chasing, lead follow-up speed,
no-show reminders, review requests, onboarding paperwork, inbox triage,
quote and estimate generation, appointment booking, data entry between apps,
FAQ answering, after-hours coverage, seasonal rush handling, "what to
automate first", cost of busywork, AI myths, human vs machine work, small
team productivity.

### Platform fit

- **Instagram feed:** visual with a save-worthy hook. Big Poppins headline
  with a single blue highlight. Carousels win.
- **Instagram story:** interactive (a poll, a question sticker, tap-to-vote
  UI in the design). Keep it inside the safe zone.
- **Reels:** founder-led, first person, real 2 second hook, honest CTA.
  Talking head, screen recording, or text-on-screen. See
  `social/reel-scripts-week-ai-in-real-life.md` for the format.
- **Facebook:** warm and plain. Same image as IG feed usually works.
- **LinkedIn:** longer copy, professional but not stiff. Two paragraphs is
  fine. Use the 1200 x 627 minimal template for images.
- **X:** short and punchy, one or two hashtags maximum.

### Post shape (use this scaffold)

1. **Hook** (first line of the caption, key words in the image). If it does
   not stop the scroll, it does not exist.
2. **One idea** (the middle: teach it, prove it, bust it).
3. **CTA** (one action, verb + outcome). Prefer "Comment AUDIT" for lead
   generation; on LinkedIn, "Reply YES" or a link CTA.

### Hashtags

- Instagram: 8 to 15, mix broad + niche. Rotate them, do not spam the same
  list.
- Facebook: 2 to 4.
- LinkedIn: 3 to 5, professional.
- X: 0 to 2 max.

Starter set (rotate):
#Automation, #SmallBusiness, #AI, #AIAutomation, #WorkflowAutomation,
#Productivity, #TimeSavers, #BusinessGrowth, #OpsLeaders, #FoundersLife,
#CanadaBusiness, #HelloPeople.

### CTAs (pick one per post, no stacking)

- Comment AUDIT and we will send you where to start, free.
- Book a free 20 minute audit at hellopeople.ca.
- Tell us the task you dread. We will show you what we would automate.
- Save this for the next time you hit send on the same email for the fifth time.
- DM us the word HOURS for the list.
- Reply YES if you want the checklist.

### UTM tracking (for any link back to the site)

`?utm_source={platform}&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign={topic-slug}&utm_content={format}`

Example: `hellopeople.ca/audit?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=lead-followup&utm_content=carousel`.

### Alt text

Every image gets alt text. Format: "One line describing what is in the
image, in plain English." Example: "Two panels comparing 16 hours by hand to
2 hours automated, on a light background with the Hello People logo."

## 10. Do and do not (the fast checklist)

### Always

- Solid blue does the work.
- One idea per post, one CTA.
- Poppins headline, Inter body, big and confident.
- Sign off with the logo and the handle.
- Plain human words.
- WCAG AA contrast on any body text.

### Never

- Full-bleed purple to blue gradient backgrounds. Our gradient is an accent,
  not wallpaper.
- Glowing robots, circuit-board motifs, floating neural-network nodes,
  "HUD" sci-fi UI, blue-glow circuit stock photos.
- Techno-babble ("revolutionary AI-powered synergy paradigm").
- Everything centered, walls of low contrast grey text, emoji as section
  headers.
- Dark-mode-only "hacker" aesthetic.
- The em dash (`,`) or en dash (`-`), anywhere.
- More than one gradient moment in the same view.
- The gradient on small text or as a headline color.
- Two highlighted phrases in the same headline.
- Any CTA that reads "Learn more" or "Submit" alone.

## 11. Deliverable format (what to send back)

For each post, deliver:

1. **The image or images** (PNG at native size, listed in section 7).
2. **Alt text** for each image.
3. **The caption**, with the CTA on its own line.
4. **Hashtags**, on the last line of the caption (or first comment on IG).

Sheet columns (matches our production sheet):

- **Status** starts as `Draft`, moves to `Approved`, then `Scheduled`, then
  `Posted`.

`YYYY-MM-DD_platform_format_slug.png`

Example: `2026-09-22_ig_carousel-cover_lead-followups.png`.

Cover, content, and CTA slides of a carousel: append `-1`, `-2`, ... in
order.
