# Reel scripts, week theme: AI in real life (authentic, founder-led)

These are not scripts to memorize. They are talking points to help you say
something you already believe, in your own words, to one person you want to help.
The goal is simple: genuinely help first, and the leads follow.

## How to deliver these so they feel real (read this once)

- **Talk to one person, not an audience.** Imagine one business owner you like,
  drowning in busywork, and just help them. That is the whole energy.
- **Do not read.** Glance at the talking points, then say it your way. If you
  stumble, keep going. A real pause beats a perfect line.
- **Use your own stories.** Where a script says "a client," swap in a real one you
  remember. Specifics are what make people believe you.
- **Say the honest version.** If something has a downside, say it. Trust is the
  product. People can feel when you are selling versus when you are helping.
- **The CTA is a gift, not a pitch.** You made a free thing that helps. Offer it
  like that: "if this is useful, I will send it, no catch."
- Still no em or en dashes in captions. Just talk like a person.

Filming basics: vertical, first 2 seconds carry the hook, add captions, end with
your face or the logo and one clear next step. Link in first comment.

## Day 1, Monday. Teach. "What AI actually does in a day"

**What you genuinely believe here:** most people are told to "use AI" and have no
idea what that means in practice, and that is not their fault.

**Hook (say something real):** "Everyone keeps telling you to use AI, but nobody
actually tells you what for. So let me just tell you."

**Talking points (your words):**
- Forget the hype for a second. In a normal day, here is what it actually does.
- It chases the invoices you keep forgetting to chase.
- It books appointments so you are not playing phone tag all afternoon.
- It answers the same few questions you and your team are tired of typing.
- That is it. It is not scary. It is just the boring stuff, off your plate.

**Honest CTA:** "I am going to post one of these every day this week. Follow along
if that is useful to you."

**Caption:** Everyone says use AI. Nobody says what for. So here is what it really
does in a normal day. I will post one a day this week, follow along if it helps.

## Day 3, Wednesday. Teach + invite. "Fix your follow-ups"

**What you believe:** most leads do not go cold because they were bad leads. They
go cold because nobody followed up fast enough. That is fixable.

**Hook:** "If leads keep going quiet on you, it is probably not the leads. It is
the follow-up. Here is how I would fix it."

**Talking points:**
- Walk them through it like you are helping a friend, three simple moves.
- One, make sure every lead lands in one place, not lost across your inbox and DMs.
- Two, get a real first reply out within a few minutes, not hours. Speed is
  everything here.
- Three, add a couple of gentle follow-ups on a timer, so quiet leads get a nudge.
- You do not need fancy tools. You need it to happen every time, not when someone
  remembers.

**Honest CTA:** "I actually wrote down the exact setup we use. If you want it,
comment AUDIT and I will send it over, no strings."

**Caption:** Your leads are not the problem. The slow follow-up is. Here is the
three step fix. Want the exact setup we use? Comment AUDIT.

## Day 4, Thursday. Bust. "It will not replace your team"

**What you believe, deeply:** this is the fear that stops good people, and it is
worth answering honestly and kindly.

**Hook:** "A lot of owners are scared that automating means letting people go. I
want to be really honest with you about that."

**Talking points:**
- Say it plainly. Done right, this does not replace your team.
- It takes away the parts of their job they already hate. The retyping. The copy
  and paste. The invoice chase at six pm.
- What is left is the stuff only a person can do. The judgement. The care. The
  actual relationship with your customer.
- That is the part people pay you for. You want your best people spending their
  day there, not on data entry.
- Speak to the worried teammate directly for a second. This is meant to make your
  work lighter, not to make you unnecessary.

**Honest CTA:** "If someone on your team is worried about this, send it to them. I
mean it."

**Caption:** The fear I hear most, answered honestly. Automation should take the
busywork, not your people. Send this to the teammate who needs to hear it.
**Hashtags:** #futureofwork #leadership #AIautomation #smallbusiness #hellopeople

## Why this converts without feeling like selling

Four days you genuinely teach. One day you make people smile. Two days you offer
real help they can take you up on. Every day it is you, a real person who clearly
knows this and wants to help. People buy from people they trust, and trust is what
you are building here, one honest reel at a time.
