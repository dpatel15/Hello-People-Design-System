# Reel scripts, week theme: AI in real life

Seven daily Reel scripts (about 20 to 40 seconds each), one theme, mixed for
reach and leads. Voice: plain, human, no em or en dashes. Each script has a hook
(first 2 seconds), what to show, what to say, on-screen captions, and a CTA.

Filming notes that apply to every reel:
- Shoot vertical 1080 x 1920. Keep text clear of the top and bottom ~250px.
- First 2 seconds are everything. Lead with the hook on screen and out loud.
- Add captions (most people watch on mute).
- End card: logo + `@hellopeople_agency` + the CTA.
- Put the link in the first comment; keep "link in bio" pointing at the site.

Lead mechanic: when a script says "comment AUDIT," the auto-DM sends the free
one-page checklist (see the plan). That is how a view becomes a lead.

## Day 1, Monday. Educational (reach). "What AI actually does in a workday"

- **Type:** Pure educational, listicle. Fastest to shoot.
- **Format:** Talking head OR text-on-screen over simple B-roll (someone working).
- **Hook (0 to 2s), on screen + spoken:** "Everyone says use AI. Nobody tells you what for."
- **Script (spoken):**
  "So here is what it actually does in a normal business day. One, it chases your unpaid invoices, politely, on time. Two, it books appointments so you stop playing phone tag. Three, it answers the same ten questions your team is tired of typing. That is it. Not a robot future. Just the boring work, handled, so your people do the work only people can do."
- **On-screen captions (beat by beat):** "1. Chases invoices" / "2. Books appointments" / "3. Answers FAQs" / "The boring stuff, handled."
- **CTA (spoken + caption):** "Follow for one real AI use a day."
- **Post caption:** Everyone says use AI. Almost nobody says what for. Here are three things it does in a normal workday. Follow for a real one every day.

## Day 4, Thursday. Objection / myth-bust (trust). "AI won't replace your team"

- **Type:** Educational, belief-shifting. Builds trust with hesitant owners.
- **Format:** Talking head, direct to camera. Warm, honest tone.
- **Hook (0 to 2s):** "AI is not going to replace your team. Here is what it actually replaces."
- **Script (spoken):**
  "The fear is always the same. If we automate, do we lose people? No. Good automation does not replace your team. It replaces the parts of the job they already hate. The retyping. The copy and paste. The six pm invoice chase. What is left is the work only a person can do. The judgement. The care. The relationship. That is the part your clients actually pay for. Automation should make your team more human at work, not less needed."
- **On-screen captions:** "Not your team." / "The copy and paste." / "The 6pm invoice chase." / "People keep the human work."

## Weekly balance (why this mix works)

- **4 educational** (Mon, Tue, Wed, Thu) build authority and get saved and shared.
- **2 conversion/lead** (Wed and Sun explicitly, Fri strongly) turn attention into DMs.
- **1 pure reach** (Sat) brings new eyes that the rest of the week converts.

Every reel points at the same lead mechanic (comment AUDIT to the auto-DM), so a
week of views compounds into a list, not just likes.
